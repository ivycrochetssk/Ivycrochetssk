The maker behind the yarn

Hi, I'm Ivy

Crochet started as a quiet hobby and grew into something I wanted to share. Every piece from IvyCrochetssk is made by hand, stitch by stitch, using techniques from simple double crochet to more detailed mosaic and lace work. Whether you're after a finished piece to keep you warm, a pattern to make your own, or your very first crochet lesson — you're in the right place.
